Hi, wherever you are on earth.

Here's a bit about me, in case we ever get the chance to do something together. Ever since my dad taught me to use Microsoft Paint when I was 6, I haven't stopped solving problems through art and technology.

While studying engineering I learned the C language, laying the groundwork to later program my first game in JS, which, without my knowing it, became my ticket into my first job as a web app and game developer.

11 years later, I still ask myself: why would a son, husband, father, Colombian, electronics and front-end engineer illustrate and design logotypes while reading about astronomy, or write songs that don't sound AI-made?

I think slowly but surely, sometimes out loud. I ask for help along the way and I ask a lot of "what for?". I'd rather fall in love with the problem than with the solution.

Thank you for making it this far. If you think we might do something together at some point, text me without overthinking it. Now it's my turn to hear from you. Ultreia et suseia.

Hugs, Jose.
