Hi, wherever you are on earth.

Here are a few things about me, in case we ever get the chance to do something epic together… I was 6, and ever since my dad taught me to use Microsoft Paint, I haven't stopped wondering how to solve all kinds of problems through the mix of art and technology.

While I was studying engineering I learned how to code in C, laying the base to later dare to program my first game in JavaScript, which by chance ended up being the entrance ticket to my first job, building games and apps for browsers. I loved it then, and I'm still in love.

11 years later, I ask myself: why would a son, a husband, a father, a Colombian front-end and electronics engineer illustrate and design logotypes while reading about astronomy? Or write songs that no AI could ever harmonize or even feel for me?

I think slowly, steadily, surely, sometimes out loud, and I ask for help when I'm stuck, and I ask a lot of whys. I'd rather fall in love with the problem than any kind of solution.

You made it to the end, and I thank you for it. In case you think we have a chance to do something epic together, don't hesitate to text me Now I'm looking forward to hearing from you. Ultreia et suseia.

Hugs, Jose.
